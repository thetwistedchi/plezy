## Plezy Retrowave 0.9.4

### Retrowave icons
- New hand-drawn outrun icons on the Retrowave theme, stroked with the cyan to magenta fade (sunset house for Home, VHS tape for Libraries, CRT with antennas for Live TV and Watch party, a play screen with scanlines for YouTube, and more).
- They cover every icon in the sidebar, the bottom bar and the phone navigation rail (including the library types, the server and reconnect entries and the expand arrows), the top bar actions of Home, Libraries, Live TV, Explore, Downloads and YouTube, and the hub headers (Continue watching, Recently added, genres, top rated and the rest, plus the arrows and the hide watched toggle).
- Unselected navigation entries are slightly dimmed. Other themes keep the Material icons.
- Row headings and card titles use a soft lavender that sits between the icons' cyan and magenta, instead of periwinkle.

### Libraries
- Tablets in landscape show the filter chip bar above the grid (grouping, filters, sort, quick filters, saved sets, Surprise me) like desktop. Phones and tablets in portrait keep the options sheet.

### Posters
- The median rating (top left) and the watched check or unwatched count (top right) now have the same height and the same distance from the poster edges, in every theme.

### Companion remote
- The online dot, the "Connected to" line and the connected check use the theme's cyan with a soft glow on Retrowave instead of plain green.
