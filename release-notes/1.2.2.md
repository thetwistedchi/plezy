## Plezy Retrowave 1.2.2

- **Pictures for people** are matched through the title's IMDb id when known, so people with the same name get the right picture. Also on TV cast rows.
- **Film series** on TV: slimmer boxes with title and year on one line.
- **Watch statistics**: on TVs and narrower windows the six number tiles switch to a compact size to stay in one row.
- **YouTube notifications**: a bell in the feed bar lists every new video from your favourite channels, with unread markers, "mark all as read" and "clear list". Kept on the device.
