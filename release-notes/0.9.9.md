## Plezy Retrowave 0.9.9

### Ratings
- The Trakt card opens the title's Trakt page instead of a "not found" page. When the Trakt score came from another source (e.g. MDBList), the card used to link to Trakt's id search, which can fail; it now looks up the title's page address first.

### Free on Plex
- New **Free on Plex** entry under the libraries in the sidebar (on phones in the library menu): Plex's free, ad-supported Movies & Shows, with all titles and Plex's own shelves, browsable like a library. Opening a title shows its details; the **Plex (free, with ads)** button plays it in Plex Web, since the service's copy protection and ads only work in Plex's own apps. Shown on Windows and macOS when a Plex server is connected, not on Android.

### Browser (Windows)
- New **Browser** entry in the sidebar: a built-in web browser with tabs, an address bar that searches with Ecosia by default (DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave Search, Google, Bing or a custom engine in the browser settings), back, forward and reload, and bookmarks behind their own button. A new tab lists the 10 most recently visited pages; the number can be changed, or a new tab can open a chosen address or stay empty. The list can be cleared in the browser settings, or kept only until the app closes. Clicking into the address bar selects the whole address. After three letters, the address bar suggests matching bookmarks and visited pages (by address or title); pick one with the mouse or the arrow keys and Enter.
- Only the tab on screen keeps running. Other tabs pause after 5 minutes (adjustable, or never) and continue when opened; tabs playing audio keep running, and all background tabs pause when memory runs low. Open tabs are restored after a restart (can be turned off). The browser's disk cache is limited to 200 MB by default; the limit can be changed and the cache cleared in the browser settings, which keeps website sign-ins.
- Built-in protection: a pop-up blocker modeled on Chrome's. New windows only open from a clicked link or within the same site, so the usual ad pop-up that opens on any click is blocked; a page can't jump to another site right after opening a pop-up; a blocked pop-up can still be opened from the notice, and sites can be allowed permanently (managed in the browser settings); notification requests are declined, passwords and form data are not saved, pages load over HTTPS when possible with a fallback offer when a site does not support it, local files and scripts can't be opened from the address bar, permissions like camera or location are asked for, and downloads are confirmed first with an extra warning for program files. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warns about known dangerous sites and downloads.
- Shortcuts work inside pages too: Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+L, Ctrl+Tab and Alt+Left/Right.
- Tabs show the site's icon. Right-click a tab to reload, duplicate, mute or close it, close the other tabs, or pick from the recently closed tabs or reopen them all (Ctrl+Shift+T reopens the last one). A speaker icon on a tab playing sound mutes it.
- Private tabs (Ctrl+Shift+N or the ⋮ menu): their cookies, history and site data are deleted when the last private tab closes, and they are not restored after a restart.
- Downloads list (⋮ menu, or the download button while downloads exist) with progress, pause, resume and cancel, and buttons to open a file or show it in its folder.
- When a video in a page goes fullscreen, the whole window goes fullscreen too.
- The ⋮ menu can open the current page in the default browser.

### Browser privacy (Windows)
- Tracking prevention like in Microsoft Edge (Off, Basic, Balanced or Strict; Balanced by default) blocks known trackers and fingerprinting services.
- Cookies and site data can be deleted in the browser settings, or automatically each time the app closes.
- Answers to camera, microphone or location requests can be remembered per site and managed in the browser settings.
- Pages no longer see that they run in an embedded browser.
- Dark mode for websites, using the open-source Dark Reader: shows bright pages dark, with brightness and contrast settings. It can be switched on in the ⋮ menu or the browser settings, and turned off for single sites.

### Browser ad blocker (Windows)
- Built-in ad blocker using Brave's open-source adblock engine with uBlock Origin's default filter lists: uBlock filters, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's list and the Online Malicious URL Blocklist, plus the regional list for the app's language. More lists (annoyances, cookie notices, phishing, other regions) can be switched on, your own list addresses added, and your own filters written in uBlock Origin syntax.
- Blocks ads and trackers, replaces some blocked scripts with harmless stand-ins so pages keep working, hides ad spaces, and runs uBlock Origin's anti-adblock fixes.
- Pages on a filter list, such as known malware or phishing sites, don't open; a warning page offers to go back or open them anyway.
- The shield button shows how many requests were blocked on the page and switches blocking off for a site. Lists update automatically once a day.
- The browser settings and the filter lists open as tabs in the browser. The shield and the new Dark Reader button in the toolbar open small panels with the most used switches.
- History (⋮ menu) opens as a tab: searchable, grouped by day, with single entries removable. A download button sits next to the shield.
- Downloads can be saved to a chosen folder (browser settings), with "Save as…" for a single file, or the browser can ask for every file. Finished downloads are marked as done.
