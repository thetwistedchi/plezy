## Plezy Retrowave 0.9.9

### Ratings
- The Trakt card opens the title's Trakt page instead of a "not found" page. When the Trakt score came from another source (e.g. MDBList), the card used to link to Trakt's id search, which can fail; it now looks up the title's page address first.

### Free on Plex
- New **Free on Plex** entry under the libraries in the sidebar (on phones in the library menu): Plex's free, ad-supported Movies & Shows, with all titles and Plex's own shelves, browsable like a library. Opening a title shows its details; the **Plex (free, with ads)** button plays it in Plex Web, since the service's copy protection and ads only work in Plex's own apps. Shown on Windows and macOS when a Plex server is connected, not on Android.

### Browser (Windows)
- New **Browser** entry in the sidebar: a built-in web browser with tabs, an address bar that searches with Ecosia by default (DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave Search, Google, Bing or a custom engine in the browser settings), back, forward and reload, and bookmarks behind their own button. A new tab lists the 10 most recently visited pages; the number can be changed, or a new tab can open a chosen address or stay empty. The list can be cleared in the browser settings, or kept only until the app closes. Clicking into the address bar selects the whole address. After three letters, the address bar suggests matching bookmarks and visited pages (by address or title); pick one with the mouse or the arrow keys and Enter.
- Only the tab on screen keeps running. Other tabs pause after 5 minutes (adjustable, or never) and continue when opened; tabs playing audio keep running, and all background tabs pause when memory runs low. Open tabs are restored after a restart (can be turned off). The browser's disk cache is limited to 200 MB by default; the limit can be changed and the cache cleared in the browser settings, which keeps website sign-ins.
- Built-in protection: a pop-up blocker modeled on Chrome's. New windows only open from a clicked link or within the same site, so the usual ad pop-up that opens on any click is blocked; a page can't jump to another site right after opening a pop-up; a blocked pop-up can still be opened from the notice, and sites can be allowed permanently (managed in the browser settings); notification requests are declined, passwords and form data are not saved, pages load over HTTPS when possible with a fallback offer when a site does not support it, local files and scripts can't be opened from the address bar, permissions like camera or location are asked for, and downloads are confirmed first with an extra warning for program files. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warns about known dangerous sites and downloads.
- Shortcuts work inside pages too: Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+L, Ctrl+Tab and Alt+Left/Right.
