## Plezy Retrowave 1.3

**Audiobooks, audio dramas and podcasts**
- **Library type**: a music library can be marked as audiobooks, audio dramas (Hörspiele) or podcasts in Manage libraries (library menu > Library type).
- **Controls for spoken audio**: skip back and forward (5, 10 or 30 seconds), chapter steps and speed from 0.75× to 2×, remembered per book or podcast. Audiobooks and audio dramas have the sleep timer in the main controls.
- **Continue where you stopped**: a book continues at its last position (a few seconds earlier), even after listening to other things. The album page shows the time left and "Start from the beginning".
- **More sleep timer choices** for audiobooks and audio dramas: 45 and 90 minutes, after 2 or 3 tracks, and at the end of the folder.
- **Only this track**: a toggle in the controls plays just the chosen track and pauses afterwards, for folders with many episodes.
- **Chapters**: a chapter list for files with chapters (such as m4b), and "End of chapter" in the sleep timer.
- **Bookmarks** with an optional note, to jump back to a moment.
- **Podcasts**: newest episodes first, played episodes dimmed (counted at 95 %), "Mark as played" in the menu.

**Music**
- **Volume levelling**: ReplayGain (track or album) or automatic levelling by measured loudness.
- **Fade between albums**: up to 12 seconds; tracks of the same album stay gapless.
- **Night mode** for the music player.
- **Equalizer** with presets (bass, treble, loudness, speech, vocals), one for music and one for spoken audio.
- **Scrobbling** to ListenBrainz and Last.fm; listens that could not be sent are sent later.
- **Music statistics** in Watch statistics: hours listened, top artists, albums and tracks, weekdays and hours. Counted from this version on.
- **Plex radio**: a Radio tab in Plex music libraries with the server's stations, and "Sonically similar" on tracks.

All audio settings are under Settings > Audio player.
