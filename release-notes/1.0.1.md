## Plezy Retrowave 1.0.1

### Free on plex.tv
- A **Continue watching** row shows the free titles started with the Plex account; a show continues with the right episode.
- Android: Plex's ads no longer get stuck on a black screen with their countdown.
- Android: the remote's Play/Pause key and OK on Plex's pause button pause and resume once, instead of sometimes not at all or several times.

### Details page
- A larger **Play** button on movies and shows, always with its word (e.g. "Play", "Resume S2 E5").

### Android
- The browser is also in the side bar that phones and tablets show in landscape.
