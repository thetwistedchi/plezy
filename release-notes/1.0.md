## Plezy Retrowave 1.0

### Updates
- The release notes of the installed version can be opened again at any time under **Settings > Updates > Release notes**.

### Free on plex.tv
- The **Free on Plex** entry is now called **Free on plex.tv**, to make clear that it is Plex's free on-demand service and not one of your libraries.
- Now on Android too: titles play in the in-app browser.

### Browser on Android
- The in-app browser is now on Android phones, tablets and TVs, with tabs, bookmarks, history, the same ad blocker and filter lists as on Windows, the pop-up blocker, HTTPS upgrades, Dark Reader and downloads to the Downloads folder.
- On a TV the remote moves an on-screen pointer: the arrows move it and scroll at the edges, OK clicks, Up at the top of a page returns to the toolbar, and Back goes back a page.
- **Phone as remote** (⋮ menu on a TV): shows a QR code; scanning it with a phone in the same network opens a page with a touchpad for the pointer (tap to click, two fingers to scroll), back, forward and reload, and a text field that types into the page or opens an address. Only the first phone that opens it is accepted, and it stops when the browser closes.
- Private tabs need a recent Android System WebView. Downloads can't be paused on Android, and websites get no access to the camera, microphone or location.

### YouTube (Windows)
- The YouTube sign-in page and "Clear player cache" now run on the app's own browser component, the same one the player and the browser use. The older, separate component they used before has been removed from the app.
