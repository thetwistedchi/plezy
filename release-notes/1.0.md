## Plezy Retrowave 1.0

### Updates
- The release notes of the installed version can be opened again at any time under **Settings > Updates > Release notes**.

### Free on plex.tv
- The **Free on Plex** entry is now called **Free on plex.tv**, to make clear that it is Plex's free on-demand service and not one of your libraries.
- Now on Android too: titles play full screen in Plex Web, in a page of their own without the browser around it. Playback starts by itself, Plex's advertising consent is answered with "I Do Not Agree" (the ads stay, but are not personalised), and Back closes the page. Plex Web is signed in with the app's Plex account (the active Plex profile), so what is watched there appears in the Plex watch history. With the remote, OK or Up shows Plex's controls with play/pause selected; Left/Right choose a button, OK presses it and Down leaves them. Without the controls, Left/Right jump 10 seconds and Play/Pause pauses and resumes. Plex's dialogs, such as resuming where you left off, are chosen with Up/Down and OK.
- A highlighted **Watch free on Plex** button sits first under the title, also for titles found in Explore that are in Plex's free catalog.
- A **Continue watching** row shows the free titles started with the Plex account; a show continues with the right episode.
- A search field finds Plex's free movies and shows, and a **Free on your watchlist** row shows the titles on the Plex watchlist that are free (with a Plex account).
- Up and Down move between the rows with a remote.
- For a show, **Watch free on Plex** first asks for the season and episode, or lets Plex continue where it left off.
- A free show's details page lists its seasons; choosing one shows its episodes, and pressing an episode plays it.
- Smoother loading: large answers from Plex are read in the background, and the watchlist row loads after the other rows.
- When Plex has no full list of its free titles, **All titles** is made of the titles in Plex's rows.
- Loads faster: the full list shows first while Plex's shelves load alongside, and the catalog is kept for 30 minutes per Plex profile.

### Explore
- The ratings (IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, trackers) now sit in the row of facts above the description.

### Browser on Android (beta)
- The in-app browser is now on Android as a beta for phones, tablets and TVs, with tabs, bookmarks, history, the same ad blocker and filter lists as on Windows, the pop-up blocker, HTTPS upgrades, Dark Reader and downloads to the Downloads folder.
- On a TV the remote's arrows select the page's links, buttons and fields, OK opens them, Up at the top of a page returns to the toolbar, Up three times quickly goes straight to the address bar, and Back goes back a page (with a menu or dialog of the browser open, Back closes only that). A pointer can be switched on instead (⋮ menu or browser settings): the arrows move it in small steps, faster while held, and OK clicks.
- **Phone as remote** (⋮ menu on a TV): shows a QR code; scanning it with a phone in the same network opens a page with the current address (editable, to open another page or search), a field that types into the selected field of the page, Backspace, Next field and Enter, back, forward and reload, a D-pad that works like the remote's, and a touchpad for the pointer (tap to click, two fingers or the bar on its right edge to scroll). **Done** leaves the selected text field and closes the keyboard. Only the first phone that opens it is accepted, and it stops when the browser closes or after 30 idle minutes.
- File uploads on websites open Android's file picker.
- The remote's Play/Pause, fast forward and rewind keys control the video on the page; in a video's fullscreen, OK pauses and Left/Right jump 10 seconds.
- Tabs playing sound are recognised (also in embedded players) and are not paused in the background; muting a tab reaches embedded players too.
- The pointer can be switched on or off per website by holding OK on the page or in the ⋮ menu, and is remembered for that site. Holding OK also works with remotes that repeat the press instead of holding it (such as the LG Magic Remote).
- Pages that scroll inside a part of the page (not the whole page) scroll with the arrows, the pointer at the edges and the phone's touchpad.
- When the device runs low on memory, background tabs release their memory and load their page again when shown.
- Private tabs need a recent Android System WebView. Downloads can't be paused on Android, and websites get no access to the camera, microphone or location.

### TV
- Holding the remote's Back button for 1.5 seconds goes straight to the home screen.
- **Remote shortcuts** under Settings (and in the browser's ⋮ menu) lists what the remote's buttons do.
- Less space between rows.

### YouTube (Windows)
- New setting **Add watched videos to my YouTube history**, off by default. YouTube doesn't record videos played in the app. With this on, after a video watched for at least 30 seconds the app opens its YouTube page in the background with the signed-in player account, muted at the point where playback stopped, for about 15 seconds, so it appears in the YouTube history with its progress. Videos watched in the app before are added once too, one after another in the background. Each visit uses a few MB of data, counts as another view, may load an ad without YouTube Premium, and is not an official YouTube feature, so it can stop working when YouTube changes its pages. Needs the player sign-in.
- The YouTube sign-in page and "Clear player cache" now run on the app's own browser component, the same one the player and the browser use. The older, separate component they used before has been removed from the app.
