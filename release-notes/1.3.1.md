## Plezy Retrowave 1.3.1

- **Free on plex.tv** plays inside the app on Windows too, in a full-screen Plex Web page signed in with your Plex account. "Open in browser" is there for titles that don't play in the app.
- **Faster start with several servers**: Home and the library list appear as soon as the quick servers answer. Slower servers are added once they respond.
- **Live TV on Jellyfin** is checked only once per server. A server that doesn't allow it for your account isn't asked again for a week.
- **Sidebar**: the "Hidden libraries" entry can be turned off under Appearance.
