## Plezy Retrowave 0.9.5.1

### Home
- Recently added rows have a **Show all** button on the right of their heading. It opens that library in Libraries › Browse with the **Added recently** filter on and the newest titles first. Not on TV.

### Posters
- The pills under a poster show quality, HDR and now the audio format of the default track (e.g. `4K` `HDR` `TrueHD Atmos 7.1`) on the left, and the audio languages on the right.
- When both sides would meet, details give way in a fixed order: first the audio layout (`7.1`), then quality and HDR merge (`4K HDR`), then the languages shorten (`DE +2`), then Atmos and the DTS variant (`TrueHD`, `DTS`), then the audio pill. The languages are kept the longest.
- Titles looked up before this version get their audio format the next time their poster is shown.
