## Plezy Retrowave 1.0.3

### Home and libraries
- Rows such as **Recently Added** or **Recently Released** show up to 40 titles instead of 20 before the "more" tile.
- The scroll arrows of a row are translucent bars along its edges, as tall as the posters (desktop).
- The audio language filter lists each language once; a language the server knows under two codes (for example German) now finds all its titles.
- The A to Z bar shows in every library sorted by title, not only in large ones.
- The watched check is a little larger, in the theme's magenta with a pink rim.
- **Remove from Continue Watching** is in the right-click and long-press menu for every server. On Jellyfin, which has no server-side option, the title is hidden on this device until its progress changes.

### Details page
- **Audio and subtitle pickers**: two menus under Play list every audio track and every subtitle with their details (language, format, channels). Play starts with the tracks picked there; on a series the pick carries over to every episode. On TVs they are two buttons in the action row.
- **German voice actors**: with a German audio track picked, the cast shows each actor's German voice, and a note beside the Cast title says where they come from or that none were found. They come from the Deutsche Synchronkartei, or from the German Wikipedia article when the Synchronkartei has no entry. Each title is looked up once in the background and saved on the device. Can be turned off under Settings > Appearance.
- On TVs the genres are on the line with year, length and ratings.

### Android
- The focused player button has a neon border and glow instead of a plain white fill.
- ExoPlayer is the default player on new installs (mpv stays selectable under Settings > Playback).

### YouTube (Windows)
- **Search**: a search field on the YouTube page finds any public video, no sign-in needed. The back arrow (or the back button) returns to the feed.
- **Latest per channel**: a new pill above the feed shows each channel once, with its newest video.
- **Sync watched videos now** under YouTube settings adds the videos watched in the app that are not in the YouTube history yet.
- Closing the resume card keeps it closed; it comes back only for a video watched afterwards.
- Less space between the rows of videos.
- Player shortcuts no longer react while typing, for example a comment.
- A **Description** button in the player opens the video description beside the video. Links open in the browser and timestamps jump to that point.
- Timestamps in comments jump to that point, links in comments open in the browser.
- The thumbs-up of a comment opens it on youtube.com, where it can be liked (YouTube offers apps no way to like comments).

### Servers
- Live library updates from Jellyfin and Emby servers reached through a domain without a port number (for example behind a reverse proxy) failed with an error and kept retrying; they connect now. A server without that endpoint is no longer retried over and over.
