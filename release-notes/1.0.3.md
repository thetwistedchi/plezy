## Plezy Retrowave 1.0.3

### Home and libraries
- Rows such as **Recently Added** or **Recently Released** show up to 40 titles instead of 20 before the "more" tile.
- The scroll arrows of a row are translucent bars along its edges, as tall as the posters (desktop).
- The watched check is a little larger and a vivid magenta.

### Details page
- On TVs the genres are on the line with year, length and ratings.

### Android
- ExoPlayer is the default player on new installs (mpv stays selectable under Settings > Playback).

### YouTube (Windows)
- **Sync watched videos now** under YouTube settings adds the videos watched in the app that are not in the YouTube history yet.
- Closing the resume card keeps it closed; it comes back only for a video watched afterwards.
- Less space between the rows of videos.
- Player shortcuts no longer react while typing, for example a comment.
