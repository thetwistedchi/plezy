## Plezy Retrowave 1.1.3

### Retrowave look
- Section titles on Home and in libraries are brighter and bolder, with a soft neon glow (no glow on TVs). Their icons are a bit larger and no longer touch the screen edge, the server name is easier to read, and the arrow is magenta.
- Episode cards show the season and episode number in cyan.
- The star in rating badges is a small neon tube.
- The player (Plex, Jellyfin and YouTube) uses calmer neon icons for all controls: soft cyan with pink accents, without glow. Active toggles, such as subtitles or the sleep timer while on, appear in full colour.
- Section titles on the details page and the TV rows use the same neon title style as Home.
- Status colours follow the neon palette: your own rating, download and sync states, the sleep timer and episode ratings use cyan, violet and pink instead of amber, orange, teal and blue. Errors stay red.
- The details page got the neon look: a glowing title, dark info pills with neon outlines (quality and audio in cyan, genres in lavender, Rate in magenta), a Play button with a glowing magenta outline, dark round action buttons with neon icons, a cyan outlined audio and subtitle picker, rating cards with cyan outlines and a glowing median card, cyan crew labels and outlined cast photos.
- Some icons that sat slightly off-centre are now centred.
