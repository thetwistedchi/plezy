## Plezy Retrowave 1.1.1

### Retrowave look
- Poster badges look like neon signs: the watched check, the unwatched count and the rating, quality and language pills get a coloured tube with a bright core on a dark body, with a soft glow (no glow on TVs, to keep long poster grids light). The progress bar uses the same magenta.

### Settings
- Choice buttons such as **Player Backend** are separate pills, so the one next to the selected choice no longer looks cut off.

### YouTube (Windows)
- Comments already liked by the signed-in account, also on youtube.com, show a filled thumbs-up when the comments open.
