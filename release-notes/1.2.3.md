## Plezy Retrowave 1.2.3

- **YouTube notifications**: a bell in the feed bar lists every new video from your favourite channels, with unread markers, "mark all as read" and "clear list". Kept on the device.
- **YouTube Play all** for a channel group, the whole feed or a channel, in four orders: unwatched oldest first, unwatched newest first, all newest first, or shuffled.
- **YouTube word filter**: feed videos whose title contains one of your words are hidden (YouTube settings).
- **YouTube audio only**: hides the picture and plays at the lowest quality, for this video or always for a channel (headphones button in the player).
- **YouTube captions per channel**: "Always for this channel" in the captions menu keeps the chosen captions (or none) for that channel.
