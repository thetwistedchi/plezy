## Plezy Retrowave 1.2

### Player
- **Night mode** in the player settings, with both mpv and ExoPlayer: loud scenes get quieter and dialogue clearer at low volume.
- Jellyfin **recap, preview and ad break** segments can be skipped like intros (Jellyfin 10.10 and newer with a segment plugin).
- **Subtitle search** now also works on Jellyfin and Emby, through the server's subtitle providers such as OpenSubtitles.

### Libraries and details
- **Languages per library**: in Manage libraries, a library's ⋮ menu sets its audio and subtitle language from a list, marked in the list when set, for example Japanese audio with German subtitles for anime.
- **Upcoming tab** in TV show libraries: shows with an episode still to air, soonest first.
- **Pictures for people** the server has none for (cast, actor pages, statistics) come from Wikidata and are kept on the device in a small size.
- **Film series**: on a movie's page, every part of its series is listed in order, with the ones missing from your library marked; shown at once on later visits.

### Your viewing
- **Upcoming episodes** and **Free on plex.tv** are entries in the libraries list: move or hide them like a library in Manage libraries.
- **Upcoming episodes**: the next air dates of every show in your TV libraries, from the servers you pick, each show listed once even when it is on several servers. The last list is kept on the device and shows at once while it refreshes. A card's menu offers details, watchlist and "Hide from upcoming episodes"; hidden shows can be restored on the Upcoming episodes screen. Also a row on Home, at its place in the libraries list, switched on or off right in Manage libraries.
- **Row design** for every Home row (standard, posters, wide backdrop, small posters), in the card menu of the row.
- **Watch statistics** (above Settings): hours, films, episodes and shows; most watched shows and rewatched films as posters; directors and actors with their pictures; genres and decades; finished, running and paused shows; weekdays, time of day and streaks; a review of the year. Every number opens the titles behind it. Filter by server, library and period; kept on the device and refreshed on demand. Choose 10, 20 or 50 entries per list. Right click or long press a title to leave it out of the statistics or count its rewatches only once; excluded titles can be restored from the top bar.
- **Import from Trakt** (Settings): marks everything you have watched on Trakt as watched on your servers.

### YouTube
- **Minimum length** filter, right in the feed bar.
- **NEW** marker on videos published since your last visit.
- **Speed per channel**: "Always for this channel" in the speed menu keeps the current speed for that channel.
- Video cards show the **view count**, and the **channel name** opens the channel's page.
- **Playlists** on a channel's page, in a collapsible section, played in a row (with a signed-in account).
- **My playlists** in the feed bar and channel playlists open as an overview first.
- From your YouTube account: **YouTube Watch later** as its own list next to the app's, and channels with the bell on "All" as favourites.
- The player account shows whether it is signed in.

### Look
- Chips (filters, groups, film series) are softly glowing pills in the Retrowave theme.
- Context menus are grouped (play, go to, watch state, lists and downloads, tools), with delete always last.
- Upcoming episode cards no longer show file details such as quality and language.

### Browser
- **Reader view** for articles, with adjustable text size.
- **Play a page's video in the app's player**: uses the stream the page is playing, also from embedded players. YouTube pages open in the app's YouTube player. The page's own video pauses.
- **Import bookmarks** from Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave or Safari (HTML export).
- Blocked pop-up notices can be turned off per site ("Never show for this site"), and turned on again in the browser settings.
