## Plezy Retrowave 1.2

### Player
- **Night mode** in the player settings, with both mpv and ExoPlayer: loud scenes get quieter and dialogue clearer at low volume.
- Jellyfin **recap, preview and ad break** segments can be skipped like intros (Jellyfin 10.10 and newer with a segment plugin).
- **Subtitle search** now also works on Jellyfin and Emby, through the server's subtitle providers such as OpenSubtitles.

### Libraries and details
- **Languages per library**: in a library's menu, choose its audio and subtitle language, for example Japanese audio with German subtitles for anime.
- **Upcoming tab** in TV show libraries: shows with an episode still to air, soonest first.
- **Film series**: on a movie's page, every part of its series is listed, with the ones missing from your library marked.

### Your viewing (Settings)
- **Upcoming episodes**: the next air dates of the shows you are watching, also as a row on Home after Continue Watching (can be turned off).
- **Watch statistics**: hours watched, films and episodes, favourite genres and the last twelve months.
- **Import from Trakt**: marks everything you have watched on Trakt as watched on your servers.

### YouTube
- **Minimum length** filter for the feed.
- **NEW** marker on videos published since your last visit.
- **Speed per channel**: the speed menu can remember the current speed for a channel.
- **Playlists** on a channel's page, played in a row (with a signed-in account).

### Browser
- **Reader view** for articles, with adjustable text size.
- **Play a page's video in the app's player**.
- **Import bookmarks** from Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave or Safari (HTML export).
