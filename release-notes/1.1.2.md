## Plezy Retrowave 1.1.2

### Retrowave look
- Icons across the app (navigation, section headers, buttons) were redrawn as neon signs: a cyan tube with a magenta accent, each with a light core and a soft glow. On TVs they keep the tube look without the glow.

### Controls (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- New setting **Game controller input** under Controls. Turning it off makes the app ignore game controllers and leaves them free for other apps.

### Fixes
- Going back with Escape from a video, a page or another tab no longer leaves fullscreen. Escape on Home still leaves fullscreen.
