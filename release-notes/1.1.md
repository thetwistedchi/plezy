## Plezy Retrowave 1.1

### Free on plex.tv
- A **Continue watching** row shows the free titles started with the Plex account; a show continues with the right episode.
- Android: Plex's ads no longer get stuck on a black screen with their countdown.

### Tubi (beta)
- Tubi's free, ad-supported movies and shows can be switched on under **Settings > Appearance > Show Tubi (beta)**. They appear under the libraries, next to Free on plex.tv, with Tubi's rows, search and the usual details page; a show lists its seasons and episodes.
- **Watch free on Tubi** plays the title on tubitv.com: on Android in a full-screen page of its own (with the same remote control as Free on plex.tv), on Windows in the app's browser. Tubi's ads are not blocked there, as its player stops without them.
- Tubi is only available in some countries (for example the USA, Canada, the UK and Australia); elsewhere the page says so.

### Android
- The browser is also in the side bar that phones and tablets show in landscape.
